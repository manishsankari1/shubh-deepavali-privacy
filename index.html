Shubh Deepavali Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026
Developer: Manish Sankari
App: Shubh Deepavali (com.sankari.shubhdeepavali)
Contact: manishsankari@gmail.com

Shubh Deepavali helps adults prepare for Diwali puja with guidance, checklists, readings and reminders. This policy explains information handled by the app, its advertising services and this support page.

Information stored on your device

Your puja progress, checklist selections, saved readings, preparation tasks, manually entered city, language and display preferences are stored in the app's private storage. Reminder times and time zones are stored locally to schedule notifications. These features do not require an account. We do not operate an app backend, cloud synchronization or a separate analytics service. We do not send your prayer selections, reading searches, city or puja progress to the advertising SDK.

Optional advertising and Google services

The app includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). When you enable optional ads in Settings, the app contacts Google to check privacy choices before requesting ads. Advertising is disabled until you choose to enable it for the current activity session; recreating the activity turns it off. Core puja guidance remains available without enabling ads.

Google's advertising SDK processes network information such as IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses these for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, and shares data as described in its policies. UMP handles consent information and privacy choices. Test advertisements can also involve network processing.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising information and Google's SDK data disclosure. Where required, the app presents Google's consent form and makes Google privacy choices available in Settings. Android's advertising settings also let you manage advertising identifiers. Disabling ads prevents further app ad requests; it does not delete data already held by Google.

Permissions and external services

Internet and network access support optional Google services. The advertising SDK may use Android advertising identifiers and basic device/telephony information. The app does not request access to contacts, microphone, camera, call logs or precise GPS location. Notification permission is requested when you choose reminders; Android may display notifications on the lock screen according to your settings. Reminder delivery depends on Android scheduling and power management.

If you share a checklist or add an event to your calendar, Android passes the selected content to the app you choose. That app's policies apply, including any cloud synchronization it uses. External Panchang and other links open in your browser and are governed by those sites' policies. Opening this policy on GitHub Pages is subject to GitHub's privacy statement; the page itself contains no advertising scripts or analytics.

Retention, deletion and security

Local app data remains until you use the app's delete-all control, clear its storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Delete-all cancels the app's pending reminder and clears saved preferences. The app disables Android app backup and excludes its storage from configured cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. No app account is created, so there is no app account to delete.

Google retains and deletes information it processes according to its own policies and your choices. Clearing this app's local data does not erase Google's records or information you shared with other apps. Contact us for privacy questions or requests concerning information you sent directly to us. If you email support, your email address and message are used to respond and retained for as long as needed to handle the request and any applicable obligations; avoid sending sensitive devotional or personal details that are not needed.

We use Android's app-private storage for local records. Google SDK network traffic uses encrypted transport as described in Google's documentation. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Adults and children

This app is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, contact the email above so we can address it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions, contact Manish Sankari at manishsankari@gmail.com.
